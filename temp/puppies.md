# Puppies

Puppies are energetic and affectionate companions who approach life with boundless enthusiasm. Their wagging tails, soft fur, and eager eyes make it impossible not to smile when they're around. Training and socialization during their first few months lay the foundation for well-behaved adult dogs. Daily walks, interactive play, and consistent routines help puppies burn energy and learn important skills. With love and patience, puppies grow into loyal friends who bring joy to their families every single day.
