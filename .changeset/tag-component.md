Add `Tag`, a short, non-interactive label that categorises an item or states
its status. It renders a `<span>` with `tone` (`neutral`, `info`, `success`,
`danger`) and `size` (`sm`, `md`) variants, and `tagStyles` is callable from a
server component. The Storybook app gains the `Tag` docs page, stories and
accessibility notes.
